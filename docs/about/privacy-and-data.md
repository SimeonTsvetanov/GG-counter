# Privacy and data

GG Counter keeps your game on your device and nowhere else. This page explains what it saves, where, and when anything leaves your device. The full legal text is the [Privacy notice](https://simeontsvetanov.github.io/GG-counter/privacy/).

## Everything stays on your device

GG Counter has no server for your game, no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cookies and no analytics. It loads nothing from other websites, and player names and scores never appear in the page address. The players, scores, theme and language you choose are kept in your browser's local storage on this device, so the game is still there the next time you open GG Counter. The author can't see, back up or recover it. Use **Export my data** if you want a copy, and enter only names of people who are fine with it.

## The offline copy

So GG Counter works without internet, your browser keeps a copy of the app's own files, not your game, in its Cache Storage. A service worker manages it. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the saved game and the offline copy.

## When data leaves your device

Data leaves your device only in the following cases, and only when you start them:

- **Share:** the leaderboard text goes to the app you choose, or to the clipboard. That app's privacy terms apply after that.
- **Report a problem:** the text you write, and the technical details if you leave them on, go to GitHub as a public issue or to your email app. Players and scores are never part of the details.
- **Hosting:** GG Counter is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security.
