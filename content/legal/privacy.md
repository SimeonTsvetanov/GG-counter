**Privacy** — Last updated: 19 November 2026

- **Who is responsible:** {App} is made by Simeon Tsvetanov, {country} (contact: {email}). The
  players and scores you enter stay on your device and under your control only; where data
  reaches us, the section "When data reaches us" below applies.
- **In the browser:** {App} keeps score entirely on your device. Player names and scores are
  not uploaded anywhere, and they are never put in the page address. The app loads nothing from
  other websites: no fonts, scripts, images or trackers from third parties.
- **Stored on your device only:** your game (player names, scores and when they were last
  changed), your settings (theme and language), the last version whose "What's new" you saw, and
  a note that the data of the older version of {App} was copied over, are kept in your browser's
  local storage on this device. The older version's own copy of your game and theme stays there
  too, unchanged, until a later update removes it. When part of a new version cannot load and the
  app reloads itself, this tab's session storage keeps the time of that reload until the tab is
  closed, so the app never reloads in a loop. Nothing is kept in cookies, IndexedDB or the
  browser's private file storage.
  None of this leaves your device or is synced anywhere. In **Settings → Your data** you can
  export your game to a file, import it again, or delete everything with **Clear my data**; you
  can also clear this site's data in your browser.
- **Export file:** **Export my data** saves a file (`gg-counter-data-YYYY-MM-DD.json`) with your
  game, theme and language to your device. Where it goes from there is up to you. **Import data**
  reads a file you choose on your device; it is not uploaded.
- **Offline copy:** so {App} works without internet, your browser keeps an offline copy of the
  app's own files (not your game) in its Cache Storage, managed by a service worker. {App} does
  not ask the browser to make its storage persistent. Clearing this site's data removes the
  offline copy too.
- **Sharing:** when you tap **Share**, the leaderboard text (player names, scores and the time)
  goes to the app you choose in your device's share menu, or is copied to the clipboard where
  there is no share menu, and is then covered by that app's own privacy terms. **Share app**
  shares only the link to {App}. Nothing is ever shared automatically.
- **No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cookies, no analytics.**
- **Problem reports:** **Report a problem** never sends anything by itself. **Open a GitHub
  issue** opens GitHub's "new issue" page with your text filled in; the text is part of that
  page's address, so GitHub receives it when the page opens, and it becomes a **public** issue
  only when you submit it there (you need a free GitHub account; the GitHub Privacy Statement
  applies). **Send by email** opens your own email app with the text filled in; nothing is sent
  until you send it. If you leave "Add technical details" ticked, the report also contains the
  app version, your browser's user agent (browser, version and operating system), the screen size, whether the app is installed,
  your browser's language and the time — never your game or player names. A report from the
  error screen also contains the error message and where in the app's code it happened.
- **Links to other sites:** links to GitHub, LinkedIn and Buy Me a Coffee open those sites,
  which have their own privacy policies.
- **Hosting:** the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security
  purposes — see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
